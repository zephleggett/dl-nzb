privacy

Effective 2026-10-05. Covers the dl-nzb apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, and the command line tool.

  • dl-nzb collects no data. It has no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • The apps keep your Usenet server password in the Keychain on your device. The command line tool reads it from its config file.
  • dl-nzb connects only to the Usenet server you enter. The Mac app from GitHub also checks GitHub for updates; the App Store version doesn't.
  • Your downloads, NZB files and settings stay on your device.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports or TestFlight feedback with developers, Apple passes them on. They're used only to fix bugs.
